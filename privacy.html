gws Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The short version

gws is a personal tool used only by its owner, on their own computer, with their own Google account. Nothing is collected, stored on a server, or shared.

What gws accesses

With the owner's permission, gws uses Google APIs to read and draft Gmail messages, and to work with Google Drive, Sheets, Docs, Slides, Calendar and Tasks in the owner's account. It acts only when the owner runs it.

Where data goes

  • Data from Google is processed on the owner's computer only.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on that computer.
  • No data is sent to the developer or any third party, and none is sold or used for advertising.

Google API Services

gws's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
